Debt path

Debt rises through 2030 before the plan brings it down

Buying the Arena Sportiva and the Palazzetto dello Sport makes 2026 the main borrowing year. The city's financial plan expects debt to peak at 1’356.3M in 2030, then fall to 1’152.7M by 2033 through asset sales and a budget correction. Even at the end of the plan, debt stays above its 2025 level.

Notes and sources

How to read these numbers

Three different debt measures

  • Gross financial debt (City of Lugano): bonds and loans owed, 1’049.9M at end of 2025. Used in the debt path and key figures.
  • Net public debt I (Canton of Ticino): debt minus financial assets, 655.5M in 2024, or CHF 10’302 per resident. Used in the indicator table.
  • Net direct and indirect debt / operating revenue (Moody's): 234% in 2025. Reported by the City with the rating.

These measure different things and should not be compared with one another.

Estimates on this page

  • The 2026 estimate of 1’290.7M adds the budget's 240.8M borrowing need to end-2025 debt, before any asset sales. The City does not publish this figure.
  • Three bonds not listed on SIX are treated as private placements. The City's public-bond total of CHF 1.0bn after September 2025 only adds up without them.
  • Debt not in bonds is described as likely bank and short-term borrowing.
  • One matured private placement (CH0536893263) has no published size and is left out of the 2024 bond share.
